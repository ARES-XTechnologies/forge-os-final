# FORGE OS: Roadmap

## Planned for later (not in v1.2)

These were discussed and deliberately left out for now:

- **Streaming apps (Netflix, YouTube, Disney+ and others).** Shipping these as official apps on a console you sell needs a partnership agreement with each company. On Linux, Netflix also only plays in 720p.
- **Console branding.** Your own logo, final console name, boot logo and colours (the current emblem and "FORGE" name are placeholders).
- **Steam Remote Play.** Streaming games to a phone, tablet or another PC.
- **EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net.** Through Lutris. Their games can also be added as your own `.exe` if they run without their launcher.
- **All-games trophy hub.** Achievements stay per game for now, on each game's page.

## In v1.3

- Add your own games: any Windows `.exe` (runs with Proton via umu-launcher: UMU-Proton, GE-Proton or a Proton Steam installed) or Linux game, from this console, other drives or USB sticks.
- Official art matched automatically from Steam's store, or the game's own icon.
- A source badge on every game: Steam, Epic, GOG, Local disk, USB, or the classic console.
- A "Your games" filter, a "Drive not connected" state, and editing or removing games you added.

## In v1.2

- In-game guide menu (Guide button): resume, screenshot, save clip, quit game, volume, performance, overlay, per-game FPS limit, rest mode. Optionally pauses the game while open.
- An Xbox-style "Press Guide for the FORGE menu" hint when a game starts.
- Premium notifications: glass cards with a picture, a title and detail, and a light sweep.
- First-boot setup: name, time zone, Wi-Fi, controllers, look and feel, Steam.
- Download manager with live speed and time left. FORGE can also start a download without opening Steam's window.
- FORGE self-updates from GitHub Releases, with "Undo the last update".
- Rest mode (power button or quick menu), waking from a controller.
- Owned-but-not-installed Steam games (with your free Steam Web API key).
- Per-game settings: FPS limit, overlay, Proton version, launch options.
- Screenshots (Share, or Guide + X) and clips (Guide + Y), plus copying them to a USB drive.
- Sound themes: Crystal, Soft, Glass, Retro, Minimal.
- Profiles with separate settings, favourites and Steam accounts.
- Epic Games and GOG through Heroic.
- Classic games through RetroArch, with box art.
- Dynamic theme: the sky takes on the colours of the selected game.
- Per-game Steam achievements, plus unlock notifications while you play.
- Factory reset, recovery mode (hold Space at boot), and visual quality presets.
